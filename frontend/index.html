📖 Clinical Guidelines & Algorithmic Foundations

Methodology, biomarker taxonomy, and validated decision support protocols

The CardioHealth AI Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) integrates validated machine learning algorithms with the ACC/AHA Guideline on the Primary Prevention of Cardiovascular Disease. It transforms 13 patient hemodynamic, metabolic, and electrocardiographic parameters into actionable clinical insights.

🧠 Machine Learning Model Architecture

  • Algorithm: Optimized Random Forest Ensemble Classifier (Scikit-Learn).
  • Training Cohort: UCI Cleveland Clinic Heart Disease Database, including validated clinical records evaluated by coronary angiography.
  • Decision Thresholds: Calibrated probabilities mapped into Low Risk (≤30%), Intermediate / Moderate Risk (31–50%), and High Risk (>50%).

🏥 Architecture & Enterprise Compliance

  • Asynchronous REST Backend: Python FastAPI architecture with automated OpenAPI Swagger specifications live at /docs.
  • Data Integrity & Security: PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 password hashing with 600,000 rounds and cryptographically signed JWT bearer tokens.
  • Clinical Frontend: Accessible, responsive clinical light interface compliant with WCAG 2.1 AA contrast standards and hospital EHR ergonomics.
